How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded MyStave.apk file.
  3. If your phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  4. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap Install anyway. This appears for every app installed outside the Play Store.
  5. Open My Stave and create your PIN.
Important: your files live only inside the app on your phone. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, the vault is deleted forever. Always make a Backup first.
